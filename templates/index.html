How FindBack-Campus Works

A transparent three-step process designed for verified student and faculty handoffs.

01. Report

Post a Lost or Found Listing

Sign in with your college credentials and submit the item name, category, building location, date, and a photo so others can recognize it immediately.

02. Match & Verify

Search Filters & Ownership Claims

Browse by campus building, category, or keyword. When you spot your found belonging, submit a claim request describing unique identifying marks.

03. Claim & Return

Approve Handoff & Confirm Return

The original finder reviews claim details in their dashboard, approves the verified owner, and marks the listing as returned after pickup.
