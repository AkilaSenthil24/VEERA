About FindBack-Campus

A unified campus lost-and-found network built for faster recovery and verified handoffs.

What Is FindBack-Campus?

On a busy college campus, misplaced belongings often end up scattered across library desks, engineering labs, dining halls, and residence front desks. Without a single shared directory, students rarely know where to check first.

FindBack-Campus brings every campus lost-and-found report into one structured, searchable platform. Students and faculty can post photos of found items, log missing possessions with last-seen locations, and verify ownership through an organized claim review workflow.

Why a Centralized System Matters

Benefit 01

One Searchable Campus Index

Instead of emailing separate department offices or posting in scattered group chats, every lost and found report is searchable by building, category, and date.

Benefit 02

Verified Ownership Claims

Claimants provide student ID details and specific identifying traits before meeting up, giving finders clear proof before approving a return.

Benefit 03

Clear Status Tracking

Listings move transparently from Active to Pending Claim, Claim Approved, and Returned, so no one wastes trips chasing items already picked up.

How Students & Faculty Use FindBack-Campus

If You Lost an Item

First search the Found Items catalog using building and category filters. If it is not listed yet, publish a Lost Item report with a clear description and photo so anyone who finds it can reach you.

If You Found an Item

Log the item under Report Found with where and when you picked it up. When students submit claims, review their identifying details in your dashboard, approve the rightful owner, and confirm return once handed over.
